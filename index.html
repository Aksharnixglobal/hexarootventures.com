Our Story

About HexaRoot Ventures

Founded by Pratikkumar Patel with fellow cricketers Jugal Chokshi, Nilpeshkumar Patel, and Bhavik Gandhi, HexaRoot brings the discipline and teamwork of the cricket field to business execution.

What We Build

We build and scale practical businesses with clear operations, strong financial discipline, and long-term value creation across sectors that serve everyday communities.

Where We Focus

Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding markets, with a strategic roadmap into lodging and additional commercial opportunities over the coming years.
