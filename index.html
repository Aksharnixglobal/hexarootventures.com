About Us

Founded by Patrick Patel and three fellow cricketer friends — Jugal Chokshi, Nilpeshkumar Patel, and Bhavik Gandhi — HexaRoot Ventures brings teamwork, dedication, and vision from the cricket field into the business world. We operate in Real Estate and Food & Hospitality with a roadmap into commercial investments and lodging assets across the Dallas–Fort Worth area.
