About HexaRoot Ventures

Founded by Pratikkumar Patel with fellow cricketers Jugal Chokshi, Nilpeshkumar Patel, and Bhavik Gandhi, HexaRoot brings teamwork from the field to execution in business.

What We Build

We build and scale practical businesses with clear operations, strong finance discipline, and long-term value creation across sectors that serve everyday communities.

Where We Focus

Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding markets with a strategic roadmap into lodging and additional commercial opportunities over the coming years.

Our Venture Portfolio

🏗 Real Estate

🏢 Commercial Investment Properties
